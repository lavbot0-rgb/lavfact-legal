Lavfact Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Lavfact is a creator tool for generating short educational video packages.

Lavfact may process information needed to create and publish content, such as topic ideas, scripts, captions, video metadata, generated media files, and TikTok authorization details.

If TikTok integration is enabled, Lavfact may use TikTok API credentials and user authorization tokens to upload or publish videos according to the permissions granted by the user.

Lavfact does not sell personal information. Data is stored locally unless you choose to connect third-party services or deploy the tool to a hosted environment.

You can revoke TikTok access through your TikTok account or developer app settings.
